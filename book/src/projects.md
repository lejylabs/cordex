## How to Submit Your Project

1. Fork [lejylabs/cordex](https://github.com/lejylabs/cordex) or click the **Edit** icon at the top right.
2. Add a row to the table above in alphabetical order:

```markdown
| **MyBot** | Short one-sentence description of what it does. | `cordex` (`cache`, `voice`, `lavalink`) | [GitHub](https://github.com/you/mybot) · [Website](https://example.com) |
```

3. Open a Pull Request with a short note about your project.

**Guidelines:**
- The project should actively use `cordex` (or any `cordex-*` crate).
- Open-source and closed-source/hosted bots are both welcome (provide a repository, website, or invite/docs link).
- Keep the description concise (one sentence).
