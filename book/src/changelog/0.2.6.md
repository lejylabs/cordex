# 0.2.6 — 2026-11-27

- **Forum, Media, Voice & Channel API Completeness (`cordex-model`, `cordex-http`):**
  - Added `is_forum()`, `is_media()`, and `is_forum_or_media()` helpers on `Channel` and `ChannelType`, plus `DefaultReaction::emoji_id` and `DefaultReaction::unicode` constructors in `cordex-model`.
  - Added `ForumTagInput` (`cordex_http::endpoint::ForumTagInput`) with fluent builders and `From<ForumTag>` / `From<&ForumTag>` for creating and updating forum/media `available_tags`.
  - Extended `UpdateChannel` (`PATCH /channels/{channel_id}`) with `applied_tags`, `available_tags`, `flags`, `default_reaction_emoji`, `default_thread_rate_limit_per_user`, `default_sort_order`, `default_forum_layout`, `default_auto_archive_duration`, `rtc_region`, `video_quality_mode`, and `icon`.
  - Extended `CreateGuildChannel` (`POST /guilds/{guild_id}/channels`) with `available_tags`, `flags`, `default_reaction_emoji`, `default_thread_rate_limit_per_user`, `default_sort_order`, `default_forum_layout`, `default_auto_archive_duration`, `rtc_region`, and `video_quality_mode`.
  - Extended `CreateForumThread` (`POST /channels/{channel_id}/threads`) with `applied_tags(impl Into<Vec<Id<TagMarker>>>)`, `tag(Id<TagMarker>)`, `embed`, `embeds`, `allowed_mentions`, `components` (with automatic `IS_COMPONENTS_V2`), `sticker_ids`, `flags`, and `attach(File)` with multipart upload support.
